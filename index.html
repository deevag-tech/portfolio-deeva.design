Camp 01 · The story so far

Engineer by degree, designer by choice.

I studied Computer Science, then fell for the part of software people actually touch.

Since 2022 I’ve designed websites, brands and apps: at an agency, on my own, and now as the founding product designer at ScaleX.

There I took Astroverse, our own dating app, from a swipe clone to a curated, Kundli-first product, and set the UI base for client apps like Horocosmo, which crossed 100K downloads in its first month.

I like the whole route: the research call, the north-star sentence, the words on the button, and the Mixpanel chart a week after launch.
